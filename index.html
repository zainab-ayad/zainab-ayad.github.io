Global Superstore Sales Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard on four years of global retail orders. It shows where the business grows and where it loses money.

$12.6M sales$1.47M profit11.6% margin

Question

Which categories, regions and countries make profit, and which lose it?

Data

51,290 order lines, 25,035 orders and 4,873 customers, from 2011 to 2014. No missing values or duplicates.

Insights

  • Sales grew every year, from $2.26M to $4.30M (+90%), while margin stayed near 11 to 12%.
  • Furniture has $4.1M in sales but only a 6.9% margin. Technology earns 14% and the most profit ($664K).
  • Tables is the only sub-category that loses money: -$64K on $757K of sales.
  • Turkey (-$98K), Nigeria (-$81K) and the Netherlands (-$41K) are the biggest loss-making countries.
  • Southeast Asia earns only 2% margin on $884K of sales. North Asia earns 19.5%.
  • 24% of order lines lose money, adding up to -$921K.

Action

  • Reprice or review the Tables line.
  • Audit pricing and shipping costs in Turkey, Nigeria and the Netherlands.
  • Put more marketing into Technology (Copiers, Phones) and the high-margin regions: North Asia, Central Asia and North.
  • Find why Southeast Asia and EMEA earn so little margin.

Call Center Workforce and Service Level Analysis

A Power BI report on contact center operations. It tracks service level, abandonment, handle time and staffing across sites, channels and business units.

2.81M contacts offered5.3% abandoned82.5% avg service level

Question

Is the contact center staffed well enough to meet its service targets, and where does it fall short?

Data

10,000 interval records and 30 columns, from 2021 to 2026. It covers 58 sites in 3 regions, 3 channels (voice, chat, email) and 5 business units. No missing values or duplicates, but the date column mixed two formats and had to be standardized.

Insights

  • Of 2.81M contacts offered, 2.66M were handled and 149K were abandoned (5.3%).
  • Average service level was 82.5%, but only 61% of intervals reached 80%.
  • Intervals with no spare agents (scheduled equal to required) abandoned 10.5% of contacts. With one extra agent the rate halved to 5.1%.
  • Actual volume ran 1.8% above the forecast.
  • Regions, channels and business units perform almost the same.
